Privacy Policy

Naama is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. No other users are served.

With the owner's consent, Naama reads the owner's Gmail (read-only), the owner's Google Calendar events, and the owner's Google Drive files (read-only), solely to assist the owner.

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The owner may revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
